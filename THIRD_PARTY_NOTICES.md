## Limitations

The writer produces a DS2 Tank container from *existing loose game resources*.
It does not synthesize valid ASP skinned models, PRS motions, map placement,
or NPC hiring scripts. Its round-trip validation is not a substitute for an
in-game loading and recruitment test.
